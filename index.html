Celebrating the South Shore
Discover the Communities of Nova Scotia’s South Shore
From lively town centres to peaceful seaside villages, the South Shore offers a community—and a lifestyle—for every stage of life.
Lunenburg, Mahone Bay and Chester are celebrated for their colourful streetscapes, historic architecture, shops, galleries, restaurants and vibrant waterfronts. Residents enjoy walkable communities, active arts scenes and easy access to sailing and the sea.
Bridgewater and Liverpool combine small-town friendliness with everyday convenience. Shopping, healthcare, recreation and professional services are close at hand, while beaches, trails and outdoor adventures are never far away.
LaHave, Petite Rivière, Riverport and Port Medway appeal to those drawn to a quieter coastal rhythm. Ocean views, welcoming neighbours, creative communities and a strong connection to nature make these areas especially appealing.
Whether you are seeking connection, convenience, culture or calm, the South Shore offers more than a place to live—it offers a lifestyle that feels like home.

A walk along the Rissers Beach boardwalk
Where the boardwalk meets the sea
Rissers Beach is one of the South Shore’s most inviting coastal destinations. Its sandy shoreline, sheltered picnic areas and scenic boardwalk make it an ideal place for a relaxed walk, an afternoon near the water or a quiet moment surrounded by the natural beauty of Nova Scotia.
The changing light, ocean air and views across the beach make every visit feel different. It is a place that reflects the lifestyle people value on the South Shore: access to nature, room to breathe and a strong connection to the coast.
